Indian Armed Forces

Indian Army

The Indian Army protects the land borders of India. It is the largest branch of the Indian Armed Forces. Its motto is "Service Before Self".

Indian Navy

The Indian Navy protects India's sea borders. It secures the Indian Ocean Region and performs rescue operations. Its motto is "Sham No Varunah".

Indian Air Force

The Indian Air Force protects India's airspace. Its motto is "Touch the Sky with Glory".

Special Forces

PARA SF, MARCOS and Garud Commandos carry out counter-terrorism and special missions.Its motto is "Men Apart,Every Man an Emperor","Shatrujeet","Balidan Param Dharma".
