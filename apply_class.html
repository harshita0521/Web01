🎸 Guitar

The guitar is one of the most popular string instruments. It is widely used in rock, pop, classical and folk music around the world.

🥁 Tabla

Tabla is a famous Indian percussion instrument used in classical, devotional and folk music.

🎼 Harmonica

The harmonica is a small wind instrument that is easy to carry and is used in blues, folk and country music.

🎻 Sitar

The sitar is a traditional Indian string instrument known for its rich and soothing sound.
